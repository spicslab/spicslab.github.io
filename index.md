Hi! We pursue research to realize Secure and Private Intelligence Computing Systems. Our research topics are broad but netted -- <strong style="color:#e74d3c">Privacy-Enhancing Techniques (PETs), Confidential Computing, AI Safety, and usage of AI for Security Topics</strong>.

<strong>We are looking for self-motivated and passionate students who are interested in Security and Privacy related topics.</strong> We have several projects collaborating with various research groups, in which you may participate after some practice period. Please first read <a href="https://spicslab.github.io/contact/">what to do before contacting</a>) and send the PI an email. We look forward to meeting you.
